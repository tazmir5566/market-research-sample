# Market Research Sample

A demonstration of how a set of companies is organized, compared against clear criteria and sorted into fit groups.

> **Synthetic data notice:** All 12 companies are fictional and use the reserved `.example` domain. The findings describe this demonstration dataset only. They are not real market findings and should not be used for business decisions. This is not paid client work.

## Business question

*A fictional company plans a payroll-integration partner program. Which software vendors in the sample are worth approaching first?*

## Files

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [`data/company-fit-assessment.csv`](data/company-fit-assessment.csv) | 12 companies with segment, geography, size, product, fit assessment and reason |
| [`docs/criteria-and-methodology.md`](docs/criteria-and-methodology.md) | The four criteria and how each company was scored |
| [`docs/findings.md`](docs/findings.md) | Short summary of the results |

## Result at a glance

| Fit | Companies |
| --- | --- |
| Strong fit | 4 |
| Possible fit | 4 |
| Not a fit | 4 |

Each row includes a plain-language reason, so the assessment can be checked and challenged.
